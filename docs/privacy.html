Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Desktop app version 0.3.2

This policy describes Personal Desk Widget, whose Windows desktop app is named Google Desk, and the informational pages under azmolfk.github.io/Google-Desk-for-Corsair-Xenon-Edge/. This independent personal project is maintained by AzmolFK.

1. What the app accesses

You choose whether to connect a Google account. The standard connection uses Google OAuth in your system browser and requests account identification, Gmail read access and Google Calendar read access.

InformationPurpose
Google account email addressIdentify and distinguish the accounts you connect.
Gmail message headers, bodies, snippets, labels, stars, importance and unread statusShow the inbox, apply display filters and open a message in the panel’s text reader.
Calendar lists, colors and event information, including titles, times and meeting linksShow calendar views and meeting reminders, and open a meeting link when requested.
Google OAuth client configuration and authorization tokensAuthenticate your personal app and renew access without requesting sign-in on every launch.
Notes you write or importDisplay and edit notes in the local dashboard.

The standard Google scopes are openid, email, gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly. The Gmail and Calendar integrations do not send email, delete email, change mailbox labels or edit calendar events. Google account passwords are entered on Google’s website; this app does not collect them.

2. How information is used

Google user data is used to provide the dashboard features you request. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising or used to train AI models. The app does not send Google user data to an analytics service or a developer-operated cloud database. No routine human review of your email, calendar or notes is performed by the maintainer.

Personal Desk Widget’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Where information is stored

  • On your Windows PC: OAuth client settings, refresh tokens and local notes are stored in data/desk.bin, protected with the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) for your Windows user account. The tray launcher’s temporary control file is protected in the same way.
  • In local process memory: fetched Gmail messages, calendar data and live Keep data are cached while the app runs. Gmail messages and calendar entries are not intentionally written to an application database or browser storage.
  • Local widget pairing: the helper can generate a private widget package on your PC containing its local pairing key. That key allows access to the local companion; iCUE stores the installed package and can also store a manual override. The public downloads contain an empty pairing configuration. Do not publish the generated paired package or your data folder. Display preferences are stored locally.

The companion listens only on the PC’s loopback interface and requires a bearer key for its data API. Communication with Google uses HTTPS. Windows account security and the security of your PC remain important to protecting local data.

4. Optional Google Keep integration

Keep is separate from the standard Google OAuth connection:

  • Takeout import: selected Google Keep JSON export files are converted into local notes. This is a one-time copy. Editing the imported local notes does not update Keep.
  • Experimental live adapter: when separately enabled, the app uses the unofficial gkeepapi library to read and save notes through Google’s private Keep service. It uses an existing master token supplied by you. Such a token has broad account access and is protected locally with Windows DPAPI.

The live adapter is not Google’s official Keep API and is not endorsed by Google. It can read note information returned by Keep, create text notes, edit text and titles, edit existing checklist items, and change pin state when you use those controls. Selecting Save sends your changes to Google Keep. The initial sync can retrieve additional note content and metadata into local memory even when the panel only displays text. The current interface does not display every attachment or note type.

You can leave this integration disabled. Google’s own storage and processing of data in its services is governed by Google’s privacy policy.

5. Sharing and third parties

The desktop app exchanges information with Google to provide the connected features. Opening a Google Meet, Gmail or Keep link sends your browser to that service. No mailbox content, calendar data, notes, passwords or authorization tokens are uploaded to this GitHub Pages website.

This website has no app-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, sign-in form or cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser information to deliver and protect its service. See the GitHub privacy statement. Other pages outside this widget’s subdirectory may have their own practices.

6. Retention, disconnection and deletion

  • Saved credentials remain on your PC until you disconnect the relevant integration or remove its saved data. Google may independently expire or revoke authorization.
  • Disconnect locally in the desktop app removes the saved OAuth credentials for that Google account. It does not itself revoke Google-side consent or delete your local notes.
  • You can revoke the app’s Google OAuth access in your Google Account’s third-party connections.
  • Disconnect Keep locally removes the saved Keep connection. This does not revoke the underlying master token at Google; use your Google Account security controls to manage relevant sessions and access.
  • To remove all app-managed persistent local credentials and notes, stop the desktop helper and delete the app’s data folder. This also deletes your saved local notes; keep any copies you need first. Removing local data does not delete the original emails, calendar events or cloud Keep notes from Google.
  • In-memory application caches are released when the relevant processes close. Operating-system backups, crash dumps or copies you create are controlled by your own system settings.

7. Contact and changes

For questions about this project or requests concerning information you have voluntarily provided to its maintainer, open a GitHub issue. Issues are public: do not include passwords, tokens, private email content or other sensitive information. The maintainer does not have remote access to your local app data.

This policy may be updated when the project’s behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
