Personal Desk Widget, also named Google Desk in the Windows app, is an independent personal dashboard project maintained by AzmolFK.

Use of the project

Use the app with accounts and data that you own or are authorized to access. You choose which Google accounts to connect and whether to enable the optional Keep adapter. Google, GitHub, Windows, iCUE and other third-party services remain subject to their own applicable terms.

Availability and experimental features

This is an evolving personal project. Features can change or stop working when connected services change. The optional Keep adapter uses an unofficial library and may be less reliable than the standard Gmail and Calendar integrations. Keep copies of notes that matter to you, and check important meeting information in your calendar.

Your data

Your emails, calendar entries and notes remain your information. The app uses Google data to provide its dashboard features as described in the privacy policy. This website does not offer account registration, accept payments or receive your Google account credentials.
